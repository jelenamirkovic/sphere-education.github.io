I am helping to organize a conference in Big Data for CS in Athens, Greece on March 1st 2024. The conference focuses on collection and analysis of big data in Computer Science, including data about student learning, data from scientific experiments, data about resource usage and trends, etc. Please consider sending your papers to our venue. We are encouraging both short papers (2 pages) and long papers (8 pages). The deadlines are shown below:

  • Submission deadline: Jan 1st
  • Notifications: Jan 15th
  • Final version: Feb 1st
  • Conference: March 1-3, 2024

For more information please visit https://csbigdata.conf.
